Pfisterkiste · iOS app

Privacy.

Last updated: 8 October 2026. Applies to the Pfisterkiste app for iOS and iPadOS. This is a translation; the German version is binding.

In short

Pfisterkiste does not collect any personal data. The app has no account, no ads, no tracking, no analytics and no server of its own. It works without an internet connection.

Controller

Kilian Pfister, Switzerland. Contact: pfisterkiste@misterpfister.net

Data on your device

So that you can keep playing, the app stores on your device: games in progress, best scores, the chosen look and settings such as haptics. This data does not leave your device through the app, and we have no access to it. It is removed when you delete the app.

If your device backs up apps via iCloud or a computer, this data is included in the backup, as with any app. Apple’s terms apply to that.

Apple

The app is distributed through Apple’s App Store and TestFlight. Apple processes its own data in doing so, for example for downloads and updates. If you have allowed sharing analytics with app developers in your device settings, Apple provides us with anonymised crash reports and usage statistics that cannot be traced back to you.

If you test via TestFlight, we see the name and email address you were invited with. Apple’s privacy policy applies.

Your rights

Under the Swiss Federal Act on Data Protection (FADP) and, where applicable, the GDPR, you can request access, correction and deletion. As we hold no personal data about you, this usually only concerns TestFlight invitations and emails you send us. Contact us at pfisterkiste@misterpfister.net.

Changes

If a future version collects data, we will update this policy beforehand and say so in the release notes.
