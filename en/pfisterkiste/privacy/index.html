Pfisterkiste · iOS app

Privacy.

Last updated: 18 November 2026. Applies to the Pfisterkiste app on iPhone, iPad and Mac. This is a translation; the German version is binding.

In short

Pfisterkiste does not collect any personal data. The app has no account of its own, no ads, no tracking, no analytics and no server of its own. It works without an internet connection. Only when you play online or import games does it talk to a third-party service: Apple Game Center, Lichess or chess.com.

Controller

Kilian Pfister, Switzerland. Contact: pfisterkiste@misterpfister.net

Data on your device

So that you can keep playing, the app stores on your device: games in progress, best scores, statistics, the chosen look and settings such as haptics. This includes other people’s names: the names of your online opponents in the games under “Letzte Partien” (Recent games; each one can be removed there individually), the first names you enter in Imposter or the darts scorer, and the Lichess or chess.com name you type in when importing.

If you choose a photo for the photo puzzle, the app keeps a reduced-size copy without location data on your device only, not in the backup either; “Foto entfernen” (Remove photo) in the puzzle’s menu deletes it.

This data does not leave your device through the app, and we have no access to it. It is removed when you delete the app. If your device backs up apps via iCloud or a computer, this data (except the photo) is included in the backup, as with any app. Apple’s terms apply to that.

Motion sensors

The dice games and the forehead guessing game read the device’s movement (shaking, tilting), and some looks read the tilt for a reflection. These values are only evaluated in the moment and are never stored or transmitted.

Online chess via Lichess

You can play chess with friends via Lichess, a free chess server run by a non-profit association in France. To do so, you sign in with your Lichess account. Sign-in happens on the Lichess site; the app never sees your password. It receives an access token from Lichess and stores it in your device’s keychain; “Abmelden” (Sign out) deletes it and revokes it at Lichess.

While you are signed in, the app connects to Lichess when it opens to show challenges and ongoing games. Your moves, challenges, draw offers and the like go to Lichess; from Lichess come the names and ratings of your opponents and of the people you follow. Your games on Lichess are publicly visible there according to Lichess’s rules. We run no server for this and see none of it. Lichess’s privacy policy applies.

Importing games

Under “Partie importieren” (Import game) you can enter a username or a link to a game. The app then requests the public games directly from Lichess or chess.com, without signing in. As with any request, the service in question learns your IP address and the name requested. Your device calculates the game analysis itself.

Online games via Game Center

You can play nine men’s morris online with someone on another device; older chess games via Game Center can be played to the end. To do so, you sign in to Apple Game Center; the app only asks once you choose “Online”. Your moves, the state of the game and any draw offer or resignation are sent to your opponent via Apple’s servers. Your opponent sees your Game Center name and, if you have one, your Game Center photo; you see theirs. We have no access to this data. Apple’s terms and privacy policy apply to Game Center.

Apple

The app is distributed through Apple’s App Store and TestFlight. Apple processes its own data in doing so, for example for downloads and updates. If you have allowed sharing analytics with app developers in your device settings, Apple provides us with anonymised crash reports and usage statistics that cannot be traced back to you.

If you test via TestFlight, we see the name and email address you were invited with. Apple’s privacy policy applies.

Your rights

Under the Swiss Federal Act on Data Protection (FADP) and, where applicable, the GDPR, you can request access, correction and deletion. As we hold no personal data about you, this usually only concerns TestFlight invitations and emails you send us. Contact us at pfisterkiste@misterpfister.net. For your Lichess, chess.com or Game Center account, contact the provider in question.

Changes

If a future version collects data, we will update this policy beforehand and say so in the release notes.
