Board games, dice, skill, puzzles and party games, made for family and friends. Two players on one device or against the computer. Free, no ads, no account. Works offline, too.

The games

One move from solved, just like the app icon.

21 games, five sections

Small, but made with care.

01 / Board games

One device. Two sides.

Two players on one iPhone or iPad, or against the computer. “Pass on” turns the board towards whoever’s move it is; “Face to face” is for an iPad lying flat on the table. Hints, undo, a move list and recent games included.

  • Chess
  • Nine men’s morris
  • Four in a row
  • Tic-tac-toe classic & ultimate
  • Battleship

02 / Chess

Learn, and play with friends.

Twelve computer levels, over 14,000 puzzles with their own rating, openings move by move and a game analysis your device calculates itself. Online you play friends via Lichess: with a clock, by name or with an invite link.

  • Daily puzzle
  • Openings
  • Analysis
  • Online with clock

03 / Dice

Shake and hope.

Roll by shaking, with a swipe or with a button. With house rules, for the whole table or against the computer.

  • Dice scorecard
  • Ten thousand
  • Eile mit Weile (ludo)
  • Mia (liar’s dice)

04 / Skill

Aim, swipe, hit.

Pool as 8-ball and 9-ball, clear the table solo or solve trick shots, with spin and slow motion. Mini golf on three courses with 27 holes. Darts with a swipe, or as a scorer for a real board. Plus four games for two fingers and steady hands.

  • Pool
  • Mini golf
  • Darts
  • Air hockey
  • Curling
  • Shuffleboard
  • Carrom

05 / Puzzles and party

For head, fingers and the whole group.

Brain training with nine modes for arithmetic, memory and reactions. In the 15 puzzle the tiles follow your finger and snap into place with a soft click, with your own photo if you like. Peg solitaire on five boards. And for the evening: a forehead guessing game and Hochstapler.

  • Brain training
  • 15 puzzle
  • Peg solitaire
  • Forehead guessing
  • Hochstapler
