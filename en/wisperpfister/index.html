In the field

Der Termin ist am Mittwoch.

Fillers and the self-correction are gone.

In English: “um, the meeting is on Tuesday, uh, no, on Wednesday” becomes “The meeting is on Wednesday.” The example comes from the tests of the rule-based clean-up, no AI involved. What the recognition hears for you may differ.

What it does

  • German first. Swiss German spelling (ss instead of ß), German (Germany) and English (US).
  • Two recognisers. Apple for instant live text, Parakeet for more accuracy as an optional download.
  • Cleaned up. Fillers and self-corrections are dropped, the sentence gets a capital letter and a full stop.
  • Your dictionary. Names and technical terms spelled the way you want.

Privacy

Everything stays on the device.

  • Recognition and clean-up run on the Mac and on the iPhone.
  • The only thing that goes online is downloading speech models: Apple’s model comes from Apple, Parakeet from Hugging Face, when you start it.
  • No account, no sign-in.
